## What this means for the replacement site

The redesigned local site already addresses several reported gaps through residential service language, question-led content, FAQ schema, LocalBusiness schema, article FAQ schema, `.com` canonicals, a generated sitemap, and route-specific indexing rules.

Before the replacement is published, the remaining high-value work is:

1. Complete the sourced-claim audit on service pages and older articles.
2. Add a concise, accurate `llms.txt` that identifies SSW as a residential whole-home water testing, softening, filtration, and reverse-osmosis provider.
3. Preserve or redirect the old `.com` URLs during cutover.
4. Strengthen third-party entity consistency across Google Business Profiles, directories, reviews, and other legitimate sources.
5. Publish direct, citation-ready answers to the four residential buyer questions CYTD identified.
6. Re-run the same CYTD report only after the redesigned site is live and indexable on `selectsourcewater.com`.

The replacement should keep the old site's correct residential identity while adding the new site's technical structure, answer-first content, and cleaner entity signals.

CYTD was then run again for both `selectsourcewaterusa.com` and `www.selectsourcewaterusa.com`. Both attempts returned the same prior **10 / 100** industrial/oil-and-gas report. The returned report still claimed that `llms.txt` was absent and repeated the same industrial buyer questions, contradicting the live site verified immediately before the rerun.

**Conclusion:** CYTD served a cached or incorrectly normalized report and did not provide a fresh post-publication measurement. Do not treat the repeated 10 / 100 result as a measurement of the redesigned live site. Recheck after CYTD's cache or crawl window refreshes.
